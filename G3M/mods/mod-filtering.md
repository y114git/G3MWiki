# Filtering and sorting

Search, tags, and sorting affect the displayed list. They do not enable or disable selected mods.

Search checks mod names, descriptions, authors, dates, categories, and tags. Each space-separated search term must occur in that metadata. Matching ignores case.

Selecting several tags includes mods matching any selected tag. Other filters, including the selected game and search, still apply. Mod tags are **Text Edit**, **Customization**, **Gameplay**, and **Other**; **CYOP/AFOM** appears for Pizza Tower content and **FB-Addon** for FRICKBEARS3 content.

The Mods Browser sorts by **Relevant**, **By creation date**, or **By update date**. The Library sorts by **By name** or **By date**. The adjacent direction button reverses the order. Library sorting does not change launch priority; use [Priority & Steps](modpacks.md) for that.

The controls include **Show NSFW** (off hides mature or NSFW mods), **Only GameBanana** (GameBanana-backed mods only), and **G3M Verified** (only mods confirmed to install without Manual installation). Blocked content is filtered too. Mods Browser lists only GameBanana mods; a mod you imported or made yourself appears in the Library, not in the browser. Content filtering depends on the supplied metadata, so it cannot identify every unlabelled upload.

Use [Blocklist](../features/blocklist.md) for persistent rules by ID, name, or category. **Settings > Library** can hide the filter bar without deleting its installed mods.
