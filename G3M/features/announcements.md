# Announcements and polls

G3M displays announcements from its online service. A message can include a link that opens further information in your browser.

Polls show either a single-choice or multiple-choice selection. Read the choices, make your selection, and use the submit action. G3M records submitted choices locally so it can recognize an already submitted vote.

Dismissing or completing an announcement records its announcement identifier locally, preventing repeated display of the same message.

Poll submission contacts the service and uses a persistent local voting identity in hashed form. See [network use](../advanced/network-and-api.md) for online features and privacy considerations.
