# Guide: record a sound

This guide records a voice or effect with a microphone and makes a new sound of it. Result: a new sound in the Sounds list.

## Steps

1. Open the data file. Choose **Sound** > **Record Sound...**
2. Pick your microphone under **Microphone**. Press **Refresh** if it is missing.
3. Say a test word and watch the level meter. Keep it below the top. Lower **Input gain (percent)** if the clipping message appears.
4. Tick **Start after a 3 second countdown** if you need time to get ready.
5. Press **Record**, then **Stop**.
6. Press **Play** to listen. Drag on the waveform to keep only the good part.
7. Type a name in **Name of the new sound** (for example `snd_voice_hello`).
8. Press **Create new sound**.

Result: the sound appears under **Sounds**. To record into an existing sound, select it first and press **Replace audio of {sound}**. Undo brings the old audio back.
