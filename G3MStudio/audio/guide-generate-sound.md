# Guide: make a sound effect

This guide makes a jump-like effect with the generator. Result: a new sound `snd_jump` of about 0.3 seconds.

## Steps

1. Choose **Sound** > **Generate Sound...**
2. Press the **Jump** preset. The sound plays when you press **Play**.
3. Change **Start frequency (Hz)** to hear a higher or lower jump.
4. Change **Duration (seconds)** to shorten or lengthen it.
5. Press **Random** for ideas. Pick a preset again to go back.
6. Type `snd_jump` in **Name of the new sound**.
7. Press **Create new sound**.

The same seed and values always give the same sound, so you can come back and make it again.
